# Carom

Carom starts with a quiet serve and quickly turns into a bright, fast duel across a midnight field. Every rally asks you to read the angle, protect your lane, and find the open side of the court.

Your paddle is more than a blocker: move through the ball to add spin and bend its path. A clean return can arc around the center line, curl into a wall, and arrive somewhere your rival was not expecting.

The amber bars sway and rotate through every exchange. They turn simple shots into bank-shot puzzles, rewarding the player who can see one bounce ahead.
